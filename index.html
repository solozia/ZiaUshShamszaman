Research question

How can we design intelligent systems in which secure and trustworthy behaviour remains strategically sustainable, even when agents face adversarial incentives?

Research programme

From securing AI models to governing adaptive AI ecosystems

AI Security

Adversarial AI, model security, poisoning, prompt and tool misuse, assurance and secure deployment.

Agentic & Multi-Agent AI

Trust, cooperation, competition, coordinated pressure, strategic behaviour and security among interacting autonomous agents.

Evolutionary Game Theory

Adaptive attacker-defender dynamics, incentives, commitment, cooperation and security mechanisms that evolve over time.

Federated & Collaborative AI

Distributed learning under poisoning, backdoor, collusion and strategically unreliable clients.

AI Governance & Safety

Responsible deployment, accountability, assurance, media and regulatory incentives, and trustworthy AI adoption.

Cyber-Physical & High-Stakes AI

Healthcare AI, IoT/AIoT, secure connected systems and applied intelligent decision support.

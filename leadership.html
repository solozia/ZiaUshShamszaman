CyberPathway

As PI, I led programme design, partner coordination, delivery oversight, quality assurance, trainer sourcing and risk management for a hybrid cybersecurity skills programme targeting underrepresented groups and SME staff.

The programme trained 30 participants across three cohorts, created reusable learning resources and an e-learning platform retained for CPD, and incorporated CyberNorth industry engagement. A subcontractor withdrawal was handled through re-sourcing trainer support and replanning delivery.
